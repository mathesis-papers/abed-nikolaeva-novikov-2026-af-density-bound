# Sharp accretive modulus bounds from Hayashi's (2001) pinching inequality: extending Abed–Nikolaeva–Novikov (2026), arXiv:2610.34388v1, Section 11.2(5) and Remark 5.45

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Applying Hayashi's (2001) pinching inequality and its projection-count form in Ogawa–Hayashi (2002), we extend Abed–Nikolaeva–Novikov (2026), arXiv:2610.34388v1, Proposition 5.44(c), and answer the density-independent boundedness question in Section 11.2(5). For every accretive matrix X, Re X <= m(X)|X|, where m(X) counts its distinct positive singular values. A positive definite density of condition number kappa satisfies 1 <= a(rho) <= n*kappa/(kappa+n-1), strictly below n for n>1. The optimal coefficient in Remark 5.45 is c_n=n, and sup over positive definite densities of a(rho)=n, also with rank-one Hermitian parts. The classical Livsic triangular model displayed by Arlinskii–Tsekanovskii (2006) supplies sharpness examples; rational strictly accretive witnesses are also given.
